The draggable light creates a dynamic illumination effect, revealing text and images as you move it around. The closer the light gets to an element, the more visible it becomes.

Enhanced CSS filters and backdrop effects create a mysterious dark glass appearance. The light interacts with glass surfaces to create realistic refraction and highlighting effects.

Atmospheric Design

Every element remains hidden in shadow until illuminated by your light source. This creates an immersive, game-like experience where discovery is key to navigation.

Light Mechanics
